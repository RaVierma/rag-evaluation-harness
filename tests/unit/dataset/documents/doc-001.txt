Company Remote Work Policy

Policy Version: 2026.1

Employees may work remotely up to three days per week with manager approval.

Employees must remain available during their team's agreed working hours and must maintain the same productivity and communication standards expected when working from the office.

Some roles may require additional on-site attendance based on business needs, security requirements, customer requirements, or operational responsibilities.

Remote work arrangements may be reviewed or modified by the employee's manager when business requirements change.
