Learning and Development Policy

Policy Version: 2026.1

Employees are eligible for an annual learning and development budget of $1,500.

The budget may be used for approved courses, professional certifications, conferences, technical training, and other activities that directly support the employee's role or professional development.

Employees must obtain the required approval before committing expenses.

Unused learning and development budget does not carry forward to the following calendar year unless an exception is explicitly approved.
