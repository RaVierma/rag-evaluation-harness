Travel and Expense Policy

Policy Version: 2026.1

Employees may request reimbursement for approved business travel expenses.

Conference travel may be reimbursed when the conference has been approved in advance and the expenses comply with the company's travel and expense requirements.

Eligible expenses may include transportation, lodging, meals, and reasonable registration fees.

Employees must submit receipts and required supporting documentation within the expense submission period.

Expenses that were not approved or that do not comply with the policy may be rejected.
