Short-Term Disability Policy

Policy Version: 2026.1

Eligible employees may receive short-term disability benefits when they meet the applicable medical and employment eligibility requirements.

When a qualifying medical condition results in a period of disability associated with childbirth, short-term disability benefits may apply before parental leave benefits.

The transition between short-term disability and parental leave is subject to the applicable leave policy and eligibility requirements.

Employees should contact HR to confirm their individual leave schedule and benefits.
