Secrets and Credential Protection Policy

Policy Version: 2026.1

Passwords, API keys, access tokens, private keys, and other credentials must be treated as confidential information.

Credentials must not be disclosed through chat systems, application responses, logs, source code, or other unauthorized channels.

Production credentials must be stored using approved secret-management mechanisms.

Employees must never request, expose, or share administrator passwords through normal communication channels.

Suspected credential exposure must be reported through the company's security incident process.
